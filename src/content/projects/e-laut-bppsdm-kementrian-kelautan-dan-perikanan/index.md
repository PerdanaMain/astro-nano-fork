  The Ministry of Marine Affairs and Fisheries will construct an Integrated Shrimp Farming (ISF) area in Palakahembi Village and Watumbaka Sub-district, Pandawai District, East Sumba Regency, East Nusa Tenggara (NTT).

  The construction of the ISF, covering an area of 2,085 hectares, will absorb local labor from upstream to downstream. The site will feature 14 clusters, each with 128 rearing ponds, bringing the total to 1,792 ponds. Additional facilities include 224 cluster reservoirs, 52 wastewater treatment plants (WWTP/IPAL), and other supporting infrastructure.

  The labor requirement for the construction and operation of the ISF shrimp farm covers five segments:

  1. Construction
  2. Upstream (including hatcheries and feed)
  3. On-farm
  4. Downstream (including cold storage, processing units, ice plants, and flake ice facilities)
  5. Supporting areas

  To help meet these workforce needs, the Marine and Fisheries Human Resources Extension and Development Agency (BPPSDM KP), through the Marine and Fisheries Education Center, will open special training classes. BPPSDM KP will offer these classes to 3,000 individuals to fulfill the need for shrimp farming practitioners, specifically for on-farm operations and supporting areas.
