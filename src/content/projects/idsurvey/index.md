### Key Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain the frontend interface for the landing page, ensuring a responsive and user-friendly experience that effectively communicates ID Survey’s identity as a state-owned holding company in the inspection, testing, and certification sectors.

### Features

- Company Profile Management, to showcase corporate identity, history, vision & mission, and board of directors with rich media support.
- Dynamic Content Management, to create, edit, and publish news, articles, press releases, and promotional banners without developer assistance.
- Multi-language Support, to serve bilingual content (Indonesian & English) with automatic language detection and switcher.
- Document & Certification Library, to upload, categorize, and display inspection certificates, legal documents, and company credentials for public download.
- Contact & Branch Office Directory, to manage and display nationwide office locations, contact details, and interactive maps for customer convenience.
