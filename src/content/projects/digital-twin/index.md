A System for Asset Health Management to ensure efficient operation and maintenance of power plant assets By PLN Unit Pembangkitan Tanjung Jati B. By using data sensors for analyze to identify potential issues and prevent breakdowns before failure occurs. This system can be ensure power plant asset performance and efficiency the operational cost and maintenance cost.

There are 9 modules in this system:

- Efficient and Heatloss Analysis
- Potential Failure Interval
- Reliability Prediction
- Risk Matrix Analysis
- Optimum Overhaul
- Life Cycle Cost Analysis
- i - RFCA
- RBD Analysis
- AHM Analysis
